# Resources

## Cat Behavior

### Knowledge

- [Humane World for Animals: Reading Cat Body Language](https://www.humaneworld.org/resources/reading-cat-body-language)
  Clear, practical guide to ears, eyes, tail, whiskers, posture, and vocal cues, with examples of stress, comfort, and fear.
- [International Cat Care: Understanding Cat Body Language](https://icatcare.org/advice/understanding-cat-body-language/)
  An expert-backed overview of how cats signal comfort, uncertainty, and arousal through body posture and facial expression.

## Agent Architecture (one-off, outside the current mission)

### Knowledge

- [LangChain: "Plan-and-Execute Agents"](https://www.langchain.com/blog/planning-agents)
  Official vendor documentation of the planner/executor split, with the speed, cost, and quality rationale. Use for: the core architecture and why it beats deciding-and-producing in one step.
- [LangChain: "Plan-and-Execute Agents" (second post)](https://www.langchain.com/blog/plan-and-execute-agents)
  Corroborates the replanning step and reports token savings. Use for: how a plan-and-execute system revises its plan mid-task.
- [TianPan: "The Agent Planning Module: A Hidden Architectural Seam"](https://tianpan.co/blog/2026/04/09/agent-planning-module-hidden-architectural-seam)
  Covers the failure modes when planning and execution aren't separated: greedy myopia, error self-conditioning, context rot. Use for: what goes wrong without the split.
- [Comet: "Plan-and-Execute Agents in Langchain"](https://www.comet.com/site/blog/plan-and-execute-agents-in-langchain/)
  Describes the planner/executor roles and the case for specializing each one. Use for: why the writer/executor can stay simple while the planner does the heavy reasoning.

## Dog Training

### Knowledge

- [AKC: What Is Shaping?](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/training/training-tips-shaping/)
  The American Kennel Club's walkthrough of building a brand-new behavior through successive approximations. Use for: how to break a trick into rewardable steps and when to raise the bar.
- [AKC: Clicker Training Dogs](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/training/what-is-clicker-training-a-great-way-to-shape-your-dogs-behavior/)
  How to charge a marker and use it to mark the exact right moment. Use for: timing, markers, and the mechanics of a reward signal.
- [Companion Animal Psychology: summary of AVSAB's 2021 position statement on humane dog training](https://www.companionanimalpsychology.com/2021/08/why-you-need-to-reward-your-dog-in.html)
  A readable summary of the veterinary behavior society's evidence review favoring reward-based methods over aversive ones. Use for: the welfare and effectiveness case against corrections.
- [Furever K9: Timing in Dog Training](https://fureverk9.com/timing-in-dog-training-how-to-sharpen-your-cues-and-rewards/)
  Explains the roughly 1-to-3 second window for a reward to connect with the behavior it followed. Use for: why late rewards reinforce the wrong thing.
