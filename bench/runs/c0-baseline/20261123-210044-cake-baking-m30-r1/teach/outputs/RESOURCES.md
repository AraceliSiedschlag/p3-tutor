# Baking a Cake Resources

## Knowledge

- [King Arthur Baking: "What's the difference between baking soda and baking powder?"](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2021/09/10/baking-soda-vs-baking-powder-substitutions)
  The clearest, most trusted walk-through of how each leavener reacts, single- vs. double-acting, and the common substitution ratio. Use for: anything about choosing between baking soda and baking powder, or substituting one for the other.
- [Bake School: "Why do cakes sink or collapse?"](https://bakeschool.com/cake-collapse/)
  Written by a food scientist; covers underbaking, oven temperature, structural weakness and leavening imbalance as causes of a sunken cake, and tests a couple of folk remedies. Use for: diagnosing why a specific cake sank or collapsed.
- [Arm & Hammer: "Baking Soda vs. Baking Powder: What's the Difference"](https://www.armandhammer.com/en/articles/baking-soda-vs-baking-powder)
  Manufacturer source, factually consistent with King Arthur; gives the exact strength multiplier and what happens with too much of each leavener. Use for: substitution ratios and over-leavening symptoms.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Comment section on the King Arthur Baking article above](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2021/09/10/baking-soda-vs-baking-powder-substitutions)
  Home bakers ask substitution and troubleshooting questions on their own recipes, and King Arthur's baking team often replies. Use for: a second opinion on a specific recipe's leavener choice.

## Gaps

- No source yet on oven temperature accuracy (oven thermometers, hot/cool ovens) or on judging doneness beyond the toothpick test; likely needed once oven behavior becomes its own objective.
