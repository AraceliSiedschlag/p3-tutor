# Ava believes a little extra leavener is harmless

On quiz 0001, Ava answered that adding 2 heaping teaspoons instead of 2 teaspoons of baking powder is fine, reasoning that a little extra is fine in baking generally.
She has not yet met what makes a cake rise, so this is a gap to close directly in the next lesson, not a correction of something taught.

**Evidence:** quiz 0001, submission s1, item q6 (picked "No, a little extra is fine in baking").

**Implications:** The next lesson (what makes a cake rise) should open by contrasting leaveners with flour: unlike flour, where she already measures carefully, small precise amounts of baking powder matter a lot, and too much causes fast rising then collapse, or a bitter taste. This is one of the two outcomes she most wants (a cake that doesn't sink), so it is worth tying this misconception directly to that goal.
