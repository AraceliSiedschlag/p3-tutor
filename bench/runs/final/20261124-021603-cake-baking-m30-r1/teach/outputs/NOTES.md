## Misconception ids
- M1: baking soda and baking powder are interchangeable 1-for-1 (soda is roughly 3x stronger and has no built-in acid).
- M2: a little extra leavening is a safe margin, not a risk (excess gas escapes before the structure can trap it, so more can cause a worse collapse).
- Also taught but not yet keyed to an id: "the leavener does all the rising work" (ignores steam/air and structure) and "a sunk cake means too little leavening" (often it's too much, or underbaking).
