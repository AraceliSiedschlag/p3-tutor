# Baking a Cake from Scratch — Resources

## Knowledge

- [NC State University News: "The Chemistry Behind Baking Soda and Baking Powder"](https://news.ncsu.edu/2014/05/baking-soda-powder)
  University explainer on the acid-base chemistry of each leavener. Use for: how baking soda and baking powder actually produce CO2, and why they are not interchangeable 1-for-1.
- [King Arthur Baking: "What's the difference between baking soda and baking powder?"](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2021/09/10/difference-between-baking-soda-and-baking-powder-substitutions)
  Trusted baking-education brand. Use for: practical substitution ratios and the double-acting explanation.
- [Scientific Baking: "Chemical Leavening"](https://scientificbaking.com/foundations/leavening/)
  Reference article on leavening chemistry. Use for: detail on the acid-base reaction, double-acting timing, and what too much or too little leavener does to a batter.
- [Fat Daddio's: "What's Going On in My Cake Pan: Maximum Cake Rise"](https://fatdaddios.com/maximum-cake-rise/)
  Practical baking-brand explainer. Use for: the role of steam and beaten-in air, how protein structure sets the crumb, and a too-hot oven causing doming.
- [King Arthur Baking: "Help! Why did my cake fall flat?"](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2025/05/22/why-did-my-cake-fall-flat)
  Podcast-excerpt Q&A from a trusted baking-education brand. Use for: too much leavening causing a dome-then-fall, and underbaking causing a cake to collapse as it cools.
- [King Arthur Baking: "8 reasons your cakes turn out dry"](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/blog/2022/07/14/8-reasons-your-cakes-turn-out-dry)
  Trusted baking-education brand. Use for: how inaccurate oven dials really are, and why trusting the dial over a thermometer causes baking problems.
- [America's Test Kitchen: "Baking Powder Shelf Life"](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/how_tos/6134-baking-powder-shelf-life)
  Test-kitchen comparison. Use for: how much old baking powder loses its rising power.
