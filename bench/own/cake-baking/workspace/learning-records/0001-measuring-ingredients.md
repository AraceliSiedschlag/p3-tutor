# Flour is measured by weight or by spooning and leveling, not by scooping

Ava can explain that the amount of flour in a cup depends on how it was filled, so a kitchen scale, or spooning the flour in loosely and leveling it, gives the amount the recipe means.
She also sees why it matters for her goal: too much flour is one of the main reasons a cake comes out dry.

**Evidence:** Weighed out flour for a practice recipe with a kitchen scale, and said in her own words that a box mix "does the measuring for you," which is why she never had to think about it before.

**Implications:** Scooping straight from the bag came up only briefly, and small ingredients such as baking powder were measured but not discussed.
Measuring liquids and converting grams to cups were covered quickly, and what makes a cake rise has not been taught yet.
