# Mission: Baking a cake from scratch

## Why
I am starting baking as a new hobby, and baking from scratch saves money compared with buying box mixes or cakes.
I want to be able to bake a cake I am proud to bring to my friends.

## Success looks like
- I can bake a vanilla cake from scratch that does not sink in the middle.
- The cake comes out moist, not dry.
- I can finish it with a simple layer of frosting.

## Constraints
- I bake in a small dorm kitchen, with little counter space and basic equipment.
- I have only ever baked cakes from a box mix.

## Out of scope
- Intense decorating; a simple layer of frosting is all I need.
- Bread and other baked goods; I am only focusing on cake.
